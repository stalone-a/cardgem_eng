Important Information

Guarantee Terms

What’s Covered:

  • The card or account does not work when received
  • The product does not match its description
  • Technical issues covered by the guarantee
  • An account is blocked due to an issue attributable to the seller and covered by the guarantee

What’s Not Covered:

  • Improper use of the product
  • Violations of the service’s terms of use
  • Changes to a bank’s or service’s policies after the sale
  • Loss of access due to the customer’s actions

How to Request a Replacement or Refund:

  1. After discovering an issue, contact our support team at CardGem.support@protonmail.com
  2. Provide your order details and describe the issue
  3. Our team will review the issue within two hours
  4. If the issue is confirmed, we will provide a replacement or refund
Please Note: We review disputes fairly and work with customers toward an honest resolution.
